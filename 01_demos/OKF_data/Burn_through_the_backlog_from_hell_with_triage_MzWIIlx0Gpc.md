# Burn through the backlog from hell with /triage

## Quick Summary
Matt presents the `triage` skill for turning messy team and open-source backlogs (GitHub issues, Jira) into actionable work. It encodes a state machine in labels: two category roles (bug, enhancement) and five state roles (needs triage, needs info, ready for agent, needs human, won't fix), with every triaged issue carrying exactly one of each. A `.out-of-scope` directory holds ADR-like rejection records the agent can use to auto-close ideas. He demos it on his Sandcastle repo, triaging issues, then chaining the `diagnose` skill to reproduce and fix a bug, framing AFK-agent work largely as queue management between human reporters and implementing agents.
