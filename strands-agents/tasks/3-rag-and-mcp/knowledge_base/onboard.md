# Onboard Services

## Bicycles
Bicycles are carried on regional trains without a reservation, subject to space.
On long-distance ICE trains a separate bicycle ticket and a bicycle-space
reservation are mandatory. Folding bicycles count as hand luggage and travel free
on all services.

## Pets
Small pets in a closed carrier travel free. A dog too large for a carrier needs a
dog ticket priced at half the adult fare and must wear a muzzle and leash for the
whole journey. Assistance dogs always travel free and are exempt from the muzzle
requirement.

## Catering
Long-distance ICE trains have an onboard bistro serving hot and cold food.
Regional trains do not offer catering. Travellers may bring their own food and
non-alcoholic drinks on all services.
