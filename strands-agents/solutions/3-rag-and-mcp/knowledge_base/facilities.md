# Station Facilities

## Accessibility
All major stations provide step-free access to every platform via elevators and
ramps. Tactile guidance strips for visually impaired travellers run from the main
entrance to each platform. Mobility assistance can be booked up to 24 hours in
advance through the service centre, and staff will meet the traveller at a
designated meeting point near the main hall.

## Luggage
Lockers are available in three sizes (small, medium, large) and are located near
the main hall. Lockers accept contactless card payment only; cash is not
accepted. The maximum storage period is 72 hours, after which luggage is moved
to the lost-property office.

## Parking
Park-and-ride facilities operate at the larger stations. The first 30 minutes are
free for pick-up and drop-off. Overnight parking requires a separate ticket
purchased from the parking machines or the station app.
