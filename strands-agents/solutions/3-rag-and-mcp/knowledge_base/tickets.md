# Tickets and Refunds

## Refund policy
A fully flexible ticket can be refunded free of charge up to the departure time.
A saver ticket incurs a 10 euro cancellation fee if refunded before departure and
cannot be refunded after departure. Super-saver tickets are non-refundable.

## Delay compensation
If a train arrives at the final destination 60 minutes or more late, travellers
are entitled to 25 percent of the ticket price back. For delays of 120 minutes or
more, the compensation rises to 50 percent of the ticket price. Compensation is
claimed with the passenger-rights form within three months of the journey.

## Seat reservations
Seat reservations are optional on most regional trains and recommended on
long-distance ICE services. A reservation costs 4.90 euro per person and can be
added to any ticket up to ten minutes before departure.
